Choose a plan by the sandbox capacity you need. Smithers picks and runs the models; model usage draws on your account credit. New accounts start with $10.

## Plans

| | Free | Pro | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0 | $50 per month | $500 per month |
| Running sandboxes | 1 | 3 | 64 |
| Idle sleep | 30 minutes | 4 hours | Never sleeps |
| Sandbox-hours per day | 4 | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Sandbox-hours measure the time your sandboxes are awake, added together across running sandboxes. Daily usage resets at midnight UTC.

Subscriptions are flat rates for sandbox capacity. There is no per-VM-hour charge. Stripe confirms the charge at checkout.

## Choose or manage a plan

Sign in to [Smithers](https://smithers.sh/smithersai/smithers) and run `/billing.plans` to see your current plan, credit balance, sandbox usage, and available upgrades in the conversation. `/billing.portal` opens subscription management when billing is available.

Checkout is not open yet on deployments where payments are disabled. The plans card shows availability before you choose an upgrade.

## Open-source tools

The CLI and libraries are MIT-licensed, with no Smithers license fee. Run them in your own environment and pay your providers for model usage and infrastructure.

See [CLI and libraries](/docs/developers/) for setup and supported platforms.
