# Conversation timeline

A desktop rail beside the conversation maps recorded asks and answers to transcript anchors. Clicking jumps. Arrow keys, J/K and Home/End move and jump; Escape focuses the transcript entry. The visible band follows scrolling and resize, including in branch conversations. On narrow screens, and in conversation columns below 600 pixels, the rail starts closed behind the conversation's Timeline control (touch, Enter or Space) and opens as a sheet over the chat without narrowing it, so a branch side pane never overlaps its code view. The rail measures wrapped labels and reduces the number of visible entries to fit its available height. Each row reserves that measured height; the position band includes row gaps and partial groups. `@smthrs/ui` supplies `ChatTimeline` and the public `ChatTranscript.rail` slot.

Settled nonempty asks and answers launch `timeline/summarize` on the browser durable engine, independently of answering, while the rail shows them: the conversation's window, which Load earlier widens, never every message the device holds. A device that opens a long conversation so starts at most a window's worth of runs; one per message it held kept its engine and its device store busy for minutes ([local-first conflicts](../work/local-first-conflicts.md)). At most two run at once, across the home and the branch conversation a tab follows, and an entry already waiting or running isn't queued again. A window's worth started at once kept the engine busy after every reload: on smithers.sh on 2026-10-22 it woke 60 cut-off runs together, and a branch page's own `branch/track` waited 7 s to start and 6 s to settle behind them, so its conversation showed 20 to 45 s after the page. `timeline/SummarizeEntry` calls `ModelStep` through `/api/model/summary`, with at most 8,000 input characters and 40 output tokens. The cheap `summary` role is an Admin's setting (`admin/SetModel`), else the Worker's `SUMMARY_MODEL`; with neither, the endpoint refuses the call `409 no_route` and the step fails `unconfigured`, never retried. A failure every later entry would repeat stops the tab's summaries, in any conversation, until the app opens again: `unconfigured`, or `unavailable`, which the step answers once its durable retries end (an outage) or at once for a refusal no wait clears (a provider's 402 for credit, or a quota with no reset: `ModelStep` seals it on its first attempt). A rejected output (`invalid`) or a policy refusal (`refused`) belongs to its entry, so later entries still run. A load whose summary model can't answer so costs at most two runs. Before this, on smithers.sh on 2026-10-25, a summary model answering 402 was asked six times for every entry each window showed: one idle chat page made 63 refused calls in 3 minutes, and production 1,206 in 50 minutes. Streaming, failures and waits retain the recorded question subject and append their outcome. The role uses the existing endpoint's permissions, reservations, metering and quotas.

Successful summaries persist on the device in TanStack DB. Full-content SHA-256 identities invalidate changed entries, and deterministic run ids replay sealed model results after reload. A stale result cannot overwrite a changed entry. Deleting a source message removes its persisted summary, including messages outside the shown window; window eviction alone keeps the summary. Summary dispatches are scoped independently, so a defect cannot stop later entries, and one stalled run leaves the other turn free. Each changed settled entry the window shows costs one model request at the configured tariff; unchanged cached entries cost zero. Cross-device shared summaries are not implemented. Provider acceptance before sealing retains ModelStep's documented at-least-once charge window.

`timeline.summary` logs/spans expose role, ready outcome and replay status; `smithers_timeline_summary_calls` counts summary attempts, including failed calls and rejected output. Provider policy refusals fail `refused`, outages fail `unavailable`, and absent configuration remains `unconfigured`. Projection failures log only `timeline.summary.unavailable` or `timeline.summary.defect`; a missing summary model logs `timeline.summary.unconfigured`. A stopped lane logs `timeline.summary.stopped` once, with the failure's `code`, and counts `smithers_timeline_summary_stops`. Deletions emit `timeline.summary.deleted`, a `timeline.summary.delete` span and `smithers_timeline_summary_removals`. `TIMELINE_TELEMETRY` keeps these records through the relay. No transcript text or summaries are logged. The engine journal retains model input/output as private run data rather than telemetry.

The compiled first-party contribution lives in `src/features/timeline`; its intended manifest declares `chat.rail`. Until decision 0017's new Manifest/PluginHost loader lands, chat explicitly mounts the contribution. There is no separate private data interface. [Design and limitations](../design/timeline/DESIGN.md), [interactive mock](../design/timeline/timeline.html).

The grant-store failure regression injects one-millisecond retry timings through `SummarySeat`, preserving six real durable attempts and the safe `unavailable` failure. Hosts that omit the policy retain ModelStep’s production backoffs.

Validation sources: `store.test.ts` (an idle page whose summary model refuses 402 makes at most two calls on the real durable engine; which failures stop the lane), `summary.test.ts` (bounds, content identities, safe labels), `view.test.ts` (states and stale summaries), `engine.test.ts` (real durable engine and replay), UI `chat-timeline.test.tsx` (keys/rendering and recorded accessible names), `e2e/timeline.spec.ts` (scripted app scroll, keys, arriving summaries, phone), and `e2e/timeline-live.spec.ts` (real-model question and linked reply on the deployment account's own branch). The live journey waits for the reply entry to sync after question acceptance, follows its `aria-controls` link, and checks the recorded question name and an answered outcome or visible failure without asserting model wording. Device actions and step chapters record its GIF. Receipts live in the work note; configured coverage is not executed coverage.


## Entry text

`@smthrs/ui`'s `ChatTimelineEntry.label` carries concrete fallback text independently of `summary`: fallback availability never claims the summary model completed. A matching, nonempty, specific summary wins. Old cached state-only summaries are ignored. The summary seat requests 1–8 words naming an object and observed outcome; its validator rejects generic labels such as “Reply sent”. Content identities, private model data and content-free telemetry are unchanged.

When the summary differs, the button's accessible name includes both the visible summary and recorded label. Its question subject and outcome stay identifiable after summarization; grouped buttons keep their group names.

| Entry | Before | Now without a summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question (`ask`) | Question sent, Working, Not sent or Waiting to send | First eight words, plus queued/not sent when applicable |
| Reply (`answer`) | Reply completed (production report: Reply sent), Reply failed, Working, Needs you or Waiting for reply | Linked question's first words · answered/answering/failed/needs you/queued |
| Run (`flow`) | Not connected to the rail | Recorded flow name · completed/running/failed/needs you/queued |
| TODO draft (`tool`) | Not connected to the rail | Draft title · draft |
| Committed TODO (`tool`) | Not connected to the rail | Title · T number or PR number · recorded step, failure or state |

The conversation query retains the existing `questionId`; replies never borrow a neighboring question or parse assistant prose. An absent question uses the reply's recorded id. The agent-status query retains the existing run's flow name. Drafts join only by `answerId` in the visible conversation. The branch's stack is warmed through the existing TODO service, and committed TODOs are selected by their stored branch. Activating one opens its TODO; keyboard traversal stays on the branch. Draft and run entries jump to transcript anchors; drafts use the same creation order as their cards. No raw tool arguments, secrets service values or provider diagnostics are copied into entries. The catalogued `@smthrs/journal` Redaction scrubs recognized credential forms before excerpts, flow names and summaries render; it is defense in depth, not detection of arbitrary secrets pasted as ordinary prose. This is a private view of the same conversation, not telemetry or a new share.

PR checks are not present on the TODO row; no label claims they passed. Web-page rows remain outside this conversation rail. Arbitrary nested tool calls remain in the run's detail, not new rail entries.
