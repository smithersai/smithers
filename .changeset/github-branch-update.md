Adds `GitHub.Pulls.updateBranch` (a merge or rebase of a pull request's branch onto its base, fenced by `expectedHeadOid`), `GitHub.Once.mergeInto` (a merge into a branch a durable step makes once and finds again by its `Smithers-Write` trailer), and the commits of a comparison to `GitHub.Commits.compare` (`commits`, oldest first, and `commitsTruncated`).
