Scope decisions

Retain the hosted MVP, public SDK packages, native crates and supporting tooling. Removed Go/backend and old app targets stay outside the roster under SCOPE-3 and ARCH-1. Their historical evidence is preserved in the issue snapshot. The repository's obsolete mythical/Cloud landing criteria do not restore removed architecture.

Inventory by longest package path; retain raw coverage configuration, configured exclusions, ignored cases and platform files separately from measurement. A source change invalidates old fingerprints. Native Node coverage of the campaign modules measures lines, branches and functions; it does not establish statement coverage, real product E2E or live qualification.

Evidence rules

No mocks or weaker gates. Use faulty Effect services for unhappy paths and real dependencies for integration/E2E. Keep exact commands, runtime/platform, source, real raw reports and SHA-256 artifacts. Retain fuzz seeds, shrinking paths and minimized counterexamples. Benchmark claims require useful-work checks, raw samples, reviewed baselines and limitations.

Receipt structure and hashes cannot authenticate a producer. Review raw machine reports before accepting a claim. Failed runs remain in the ledger; only a later qualifying result for the same cell resolves that cell. Local and live results remain separate.
